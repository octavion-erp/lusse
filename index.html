Our story

A Dubai atelier built on one rule: never strip the hair.

LUSSÉ began in 2016 in a small operation in Dubai, out of frustration with an industry that sold "human hair" that matted within a month. We searched for the real thing — 100% human hair, ethically donated ponytails kept cuticle-intact, never acid-processed — and built a studio around matching it by hand.

Today we colour-match, cut and fit for clients across Dubai and the wider UAE, from clip-ins for a wedding to keratin bonds worn for months. Every set is single-donor, so the texture and colour behave as one. And because the hair is genuinely untreated, your own colourist can tone and gloss it to sit invisibly beside your hair.

We're small on purpose. You'll meet the same stylist who matched your colour when you come back for your move-up, and the same team answers your WhatsApp. That continuity is how extensions end up lasting nine to twelve months instead of a season.

The five-step ritual

Cared for like your own, they last twice as long.

i

Cleanse

Sulfate-free wash worked downward from the weft. Never scrub the bonds.

ii

Condition

Mid-lengths to ends only — keep it away from tapes and tips.

iii

Detangle

A looped brush from the ends up, holding the weft to spare the bond.

iv

Dry

Air-dry to 80%, then a cool blow-out. Heat protectant before any iron.

v

Sleep

A loose braid on a silk pillowcase — the habit that doubles wear.
